# Example UCB Retail Loan Policy v3

Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd.\
Policy reference: EUCB/CRD/POL/RL/03\
Version: 3.0\
Approved by the Board of Directors: 24 March 2023 (Resolution No. BOD/2023/41)\
Effective date: 1 April 2023\
Policy owner: Credit Department, Head Office

## 1 Introduction and scope

1.1 This Retail Loan Policy ("the Policy") sets out the framework within which Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd. ("the Bank") shall appraise, sanction, disburse, monitor and recover retail loans.

1.2 The Policy applies to all retail term loans and demand loans sanctioned by the branches of the Bank, including housing loans, vehicle loans, personal loans, education loans, loans against deposits and gold loans, and to loans to individuals for business purposes.

1.3 The Policy shall be read together with the Loan Policy, the Interest Rate Policy, the KYC and AML Policy, the Fair Practices Code and the Customer Rights Policy of the Bank, and with the instructions issued by the Reserve Bank of India and the Registrar of Co-operative Societies from time to time. In case of any inconsistency, the regulatory instruction shall prevail.

1.4 This version supersedes version 2.1 of the Policy dated 1 April 2021.

## 2 Definitions

2.1 "Bank" means Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd., a primary (urban) co-operative bank holding a banking licence issued by the Reserve Bank of India.

2.2 "Borrower" means a member of the Bank, including a nominal member, who has availed of a loan under this Policy.

2.3 "KFS" means the Key Facts Statement, a standardised statement of the key terms of a loan, including the annual percentage rate (APR), the fees and charges and the repayment schedule, provided to the borrower before execution of the loan contract.

2.4 "MSE" means a Micro or Small Enterprise as classified under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 and the notifications issued thereunder.

2.5 "Tier 3 UCB" means an urban co-operative bank classified as Tier 3 under the revised regulatory framework for urban co-operative banks issued by the Reserve Bank of India, that is, a bank with deposits of more than ₹1,000 crore and up to ₹10,000 crore. The Bank is a Tier 3 UCB.

2.6 "Floating rate loan" means a loan on which the rate of interest is linked to an external or internal benchmark and is reset periodically during the tenor of the loan.

2.7 "Pre-payment" or "pre-closure" means repayment of a loan, in full or in part, before its scheduled due date; "foreclosure" means pre-payment of the loan in full.

2.8 "EMI" means the equated monthly instalment payable by the borrower towards interest and principal.

2.9 "Overdue instalment" means an EMI or other instalment that has not been paid in full on its due date.

2.10 "Sanctioning Authority" means the official or committee of the Bank empowered under the Delegation of Financial Powers to sanction a loan.

## 3 Eligibility

3.1 A loan under this Policy may be granted only to a member of the Bank who is at least 21 years of age at the time of application. The loan shall be repayable before the borrower attains 70 years of age, unless an eligible co-borrower is joined.

3.2 The repaying capacity of the borrower shall be assessed on the basis of documented income. The total of all EMIs, including the proposed EMI, shall not ordinarily exceed 60% of the net monthly income of the borrower.

3.3 A credit information report shall be obtained from at least one credit information company for every borrower, co-borrower and guarantor before sanction.

3.4 Customer due diligence shall be completed in terms of the KYC and AML Policy of the Bank before the account is opened.

## 4 Loan products and limits

4.1 Housing loans may be sanctioned for purchase or construction of a residential house, purchase of a residential flat, or repairs and extension of an existing house, subject to the individual housing loan limits prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India for urban co-operative banks.

4.2 Vehicle loans may be sanctioned for purchase of new two-wheelers and four-wheelers for personal use, up to 85% of the on-road price of the vehicle.

4.3 Personal loans may be sanctioned to salaried members with a minimum of two years of continuous service, up to a maximum tenor of 60 months.

4.4 Loans to individuals for business purposes, including loans to proprietors of MSEs, shall be appraised on the basis of the cash flows of the business and shall be subject to the exposure limits in the Loan Policy.

4.5 Gold loans shall be governed by the Gold Loan Operational Guidelines of the Bank, which shall be read with this Policy.

## 5 Interest rates

5.1 Rates of interest on retail loans shall be determined in accordance with the Interest Rate Policy approved by the Board of Directors.

5.2 Floating rate retail loans shall be linked to the External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR) of the Bank, which is linked to the policy repo rate of the Reserve Bank of India. The spread over the benchmark shall be fixed at the time of sanction.

5.3 Interest shall be computed on daily balances and applied with monthly rests.

5.4 Any change in the benchmark rate shall be passed on to the borrower from the next reset date, and the borrower shall be informed of the change through SMS, e-mail or letter.

## 6 Floating rate loans and reset of interest

6.1 The interest rate on floating rate loans shall be reset at least once in three months.

6.2 At the time of sanction, the Bank shall clearly communicate to the borrower the possible impact of a change in the benchmark rate on the EMI, the tenor or both.

6.3 At the time of reset of the floating rate, the Bank may, at its option, offer the borrower a choice to switch to a fixed rate as per its Board approved policy.

6.4 At the time of reset, the borrower shall be given the choice to opt for (a) enhancement in EMI, (b) elongation of tenor, or (c) a combination of both, subject to the age limit in clause 3.1.

6.5 The Bank shall ensure that elongation of tenor does not result in negative amortisation of the loan.

6.6 A statement shall be made available to the borrower at the end of each quarter showing the principal and interest recovered, the EMI amount, the number of EMIs left and the annualised rate of interest.

## 7 Pre-payment and foreclosure

7.1 A borrower may pre-pay the loan, in full or in part, by giving written notice of at least seven days to the branch.

7.2 Part pre-payments shall be adjusted against the principal outstanding, and the EMI or the remaining tenor shall be reduced at the option of the borrower.

7.3 On full repayment, the Bank shall release all original property documents and file for removal of charges registered with any registry within 30 days.

7.4 Foreclosure charges of 2% plus GST on the principal outstanding shall be levied on all term loans pre-closed by the borrower.

7.5 Where the loan is taken over by another lender, the branch shall issue the foreclosure statement within seven working days of receiving a written request.

## 8 Overdue, default and recovery

8.1 An instalment not paid on its due date shall be treated as overdue, and the account shall be classified as a special mention account or a non-performing asset in accordance with the prudential norms of the Reserve Bank of India.

8.2 Penal interest at 2% per month over the applicable rate of interest shall be charged on overdue instalments and compounded monthly.

8.3 The branch shall send a reminder to the borrower by SMS or letter within seven days of an instalment becoming overdue.

8.4 Recovery shall be carried out in accordance with the Fair Practices Code of the Bank. No borrower shall be subjected to harassment, intimidation or calls at odd hours.

8.5 Where an account is classified as a non-performing asset, recovery may be initiated under the applicable law, including the provisions for enforcement of security interest, after giving the borrower due notice.

## 9 Fees and charges

9.1 Processing fee and documentation charges shall be recovered as applicable at the time of sanction.

9.2 The schedule of service charges approved by the Board shall be displayed at all branches and on the website of the Bank.

9.3 Third-party charges such as stamp duty, registration fees, valuation fees and legal fees shall be recovered at actuals.

9.4 Goods and Services Tax shall be recovered on fees and charges as applicable.

## 10 Disclosure and documentation

10.1 Every sanction shall be communicated to the borrower in writing, in a language understood by the borrower, setting out the amount, the rate of interest, the tenor and the fees and charges.

10.2 A KFS shall be provided to the borrower before the execution of the loan agreement.

10.3 A copy of the loan agreement, together with a copy of every enclosure quoted in the agreement, shall be furnished to the borrower at the time of sanction or disbursement.

## 11 Monitoring

11.1 Branches shall monitor end-use of funds, timely creation of security and renewal of insurance.

11.2 The Credit Monitoring Cell at the Head Office shall review all retail accounts showing overdues of more than 30 days every month.

## 12 Grievance redress

12.1 Complaints relating to retail loans shall be handled in accordance with the Grievance Redressal Policy of the Bank.

12.2 The name and contact details of the Grievance Redressal Officer shall be displayed at every branch and on the website of the Bank.

## 13 Review of the Policy

13.1 The Policy shall be reviewed by the Board of Directors at least once a year, or earlier if required by a change in regulation.
