# Sanction Letters – MSE business loans

## Letter A – Sanction of term loan

Ref. No.: EXUCB/ENB/MSE/SL/2026/0031\
Date: 15 March 2026

To\
Mr. Anil Prakash Deshmukh\
Proprietor, M/s Sample Precision Works\
Plot No. 7, Example Industrial Estate, Pune 411 000\
Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-XX-00-0000001 (Micro Enterprise)

Dear Sir,

Subject: Sanction of term loan of ₹40,00,000 for purchase of machinery

We are pleased to inform you that the Bank has sanctioned the facility described below to you, in your capacity as the sole proprietor of M/s Sample Precision Works, on the following terms and conditions.

1.1 Borrower: Mr. Anil Prakash Deshmukh, an individual, proprietor of M/s Sample Precision Works, a Micro Enterprise.

1.2 Facility: Term loan.

1.3 Purpose: Purchase of two CNC turning machines for the business of the proprietorship (business purpose).

1.4 Sanctioned amount: ₹40,00,000 (Rupees Forty Lakh only).

1.5 Date of sanction: 15 March 2026.

1.6 Rate of interest: 9.75% per annum, floating, linked to the External Benchmark Lending Rate of the Bank, reset quarterly.

1.7 Tenor and repayment: 84 months, including a moratorium of 6 months, repayable in 78 equated monthly instalments.

1.8 Security: Hypothecation of the machinery purchased out of the loan, and personal guarantee of Mrs. Sunita Anil Deshmukh.

2.1 Processing fee: 0.50% of the sanctioned amount, plus GST.

2.2 Pre-payment charges: 2% of the amount prepaid.

2.3 Penal charges: 1% per annum on the amount in default, for the period of default; no interest shall be computed on penal charges.

2.4 Other conditions: The borrower shall submit stock and asset statements every quarter and shall route all business transactions through the current account with the Bank.

2.5 A Key Facts Statement for this facility is enclosed. Please sign and return the duplicate copy of this letter in token of acceptance within 15 days.

Yours faithfully,\
For Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd.\
Branch Manager, Example Nagar Branch
