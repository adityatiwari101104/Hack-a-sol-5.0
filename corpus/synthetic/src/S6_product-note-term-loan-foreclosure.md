# Product Note – Term Loan Foreclosure

Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd.\
Product note reference: EUCB/CRD/PN/TL/07\
Issued by: Credit Department, Head Office\
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1 Purpose

1.1 This product note sets out the charges and the procedure applicable when a borrower forecloses a term loan, that is, repays the entire outstanding balance before the scheduled maturity date.

1.2 Branches shall follow this note together with the Retail Loan Policy and the instructions of the Reserve Bank of India in force from time to time.

## 2 Scope

2.1 This note applies to term loans sanctioned or renewed on or after 1 January 2026.

2.2 This note does not apply to loans to Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs), including loans to their proprietors, which are dealt with in the respective sanction letters.

## 3 Foreclosure charges

3.1 For non-individual (corporate) borrowers, a foreclosure charge of 2% of the principal outstanding applies.

3.2 For individual borrowers on floating rate loans for non-business purposes, no foreclosure charge applies.

3.3 The applicable charge, if any, shall be stated in the Key Facts Statement and the sanction letter. No charge that is not so disclosed shall be recovered.

## 4 Procedure

4.1 The borrower shall submit a written request for foreclosure at the branch where the loan account is maintained.

4.2 The branch shall issue a foreclosure statement showing the principal outstanding, interest up to the date of foreclosure and the applicable charge, if any, within three working days of the request.

4.3 On receipt of the full amount, the branch shall close the loan account, issue a no-dues certificate and release all original documents within 30 days.

4.4 Branches shall record the reason for foreclosure, where furnished by the borrower, for analysis by the Head Office.
