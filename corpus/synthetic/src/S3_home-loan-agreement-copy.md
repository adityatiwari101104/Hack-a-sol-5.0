# Home Loan Agreement (copy)

Loan Account No.: EXUCB/HL/2026/000417\
Branch: Example Nagar Branch, Pune\
Date of agreement: 10 February 2026\
Place of execution: Pune

This Home Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is made at Pune on 10 February 2026 between Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd., a co-operative society registered under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, carrying on banking business under a licence issued by the Reserve Bank of India, having its registered office at 1 Sample Road, Example Nagar, Pune 411 000 (hereinafter called "the Bank", which expression shall include its successors and assigns), of the One Part; and Mr. Ravi Kumar Sharma, son of Mr. Suresh Kumar Sharma, aged 38 years, PAN ABCPS1234K, residing at Flat No. 12, Sample Residency, 4th Lane, Example Nagar, Pune 411 000, a member of the Bank holding Membership No. M-004521 (hereinafter called "the Borrower", which expression shall include his heirs, executors and administrators), of the Other Part.

WHEREAS (A) the Borrower has applied to the Bank for a loan for the purchase of a residential flat described in Schedule I; (B) the Bank has, by its sanction letter No. EXUCB/ENB/HL/SL/2026/0089 dated 10 February 2026, agreed to grant the loan on the terms set out in this Agreement; and (C) the Borrower has received and accepted the Key Facts Statement for the loan before executing this Agreement.

NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows.

## 1 Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires: (a) "Loan" means the loan of ₹25,00,000 granted under this Agreement, or so much of it as is outstanding from time to time; (b) "EMI" means the equated monthly instalment specified in Schedule I; (c) "Due Date" means the 10th day of each calendar month; (d) "Property" means the residential flat described in Schedule I; (e) "KFS" means the Key Facts Statement furnished to the Borrower for the Loan; and (f) "Outstanding Balance" means the principal, interest and other amounts payable under this Agreement that remain unpaid.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

1.3 The Schedules form an integral part of this Agreement. In case of any inconsistency between the body of this Agreement and the KFS, the terms more favourable to the Borrower shall apply.

## 2 The Loan

2.1 The Bank agrees to lend to the Borrower, and the Borrower agrees to borrow from the Bank, a term loan of ₹25,00,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Lakh only) on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

2.2 The Loan is granted for the purchase of a residential house, being the Property, for the self-occupation of the Borrower and his family. The Loan is not granted for any business purpose, and the Borrower shall not use the Loan or any part of it for any business, speculative or unlawful purpose.

2.3 The Loan was sanctioned on 10 February 2026.

2.4 The Borrower confirms that the information furnished in the loan application and the documents submitted in support of it are true, complete and correct.

## 3 Interest

3.1 The Loan carries a floating rate of interest. The rate of interest applicable on the date of sanction is 8.50% per annum, being the External Benchmark Lending Rate of the Bank (linked to the policy repo rate of the Reserve Bank of India) plus the spread stated in the sanction letter.

3.2 Interest shall be calculated on the daily reducing balance of the Loan and applied with monthly rests.

3.3 The spread shall remain unchanged during the tenor of the Loan, except where the credit assessment of the Borrower undergoes a substantial change and the change is agreed upon in the loan contract or communicated to the Borrower in advance.

3.4 Any change in the benchmark rate shall be passed on to the Borrower in full on the next reset date, whether the change is an increase or a decrease.

## 4 Disbursement

4.1 The Loan shall be disbursed only after (a) execution of this Agreement and the security documents, (b) submission of the title documents of the Property, (c) payment of the Borrower's own contribution towards the cost of the Property, and (d) completion of any other condition stated in the sanction letter.

4.2 The Loan shall be disbursed by direct credit to the bank account of the seller of the Property, in one or more tranches. The Bank shall not disburse any part of the Loan in cash.

4.3 The Borrower shall bear interest on each tranche from the date of its disbursement. Interest on amounts disbursed before the commencement of EMIs (pre-EMI interest) shall be paid on the Due Date of each month.

4.4 The Bank may decline to disburse any undrawn part of the Loan if an event of default has occurred or if, in the reasonable opinion of the Bank, there has been a material adverse change in the financial position of the Borrower. The Bank shall give written reasons for any such decision.

## 5 Repayment

5.1 The Loan shall be repaid in 240 monthly instalments. The EMI on the date of sanction is ₹21,696. The first EMI shall fall due on the Due Date of the month following the month in which the Loan is fully disbursed.

5.2 EMIs shall be paid by NACH mandate debiting the Borrower's Savings Account No. 0102-SB-0045678 maintained with the Bank's Example Nagar Branch (IFSC EXUC0001234). The Borrower shall keep sufficient balance in the account on every Due Date.

5.3 Every payment received from the Borrower shall be appropriated in the following order: (a) costs and charges, (b) penal charges, (c) interest, and (d) principal.

5.4 The Bank shall provide the Borrower a repayment schedule at the time of first disbursement and a revised schedule after every reset or part pre-payment.

## 6 Reset of interest rate

6.1 The rate of interest shall be reset on the first day of every quarter (January, April, July and October) in line with the benchmark rate prevailing on that date.

6.2 Any increase in the EMI, the tenor or both on account of a reset shall be communicated to the Borrower immediately through SMS, e-mail or letter.

6.3 At the time of reset of the rate of interest, the Bank may, at its option, offer the Borrower a choice to switch over to a fixed rate of interest as per its Board approved policy. The charge for such a switch is stated in Schedule II.

6.4 At the time of reset, the Borrower may opt for (a) enhancement of the EMI, (b) elongation of the tenor, or (c) a combination of both. Elongation of the tenor shall be subject to the Borrower attaining not more than 70 years of age at the end of the revised tenor.

6.5 The Bank shall ensure that elongation of the tenor does not result in negative amortisation of the Loan.

6.6 The Bank shall make available to the Borrower, at the end of each quarter, a statement showing the principal and interest recovered till date, the EMI amount, the number of EMIs left and the annualised rate of interest for the entire tenor of the Loan.

## 7 Security

7.1 The Loan, together with interest, charges and other amounts payable under this Agreement, shall be secured by a registered mortgage of the Property in favour of the Bank.

7.2 The Borrower shall deposit with the Bank the original title documents of the Property listed in the sanction letter and shall execute such further documents as the Bank may reasonably require to create and perfect the security.

7.3 The Bank shall register the security interest with the Central Registry within the period prescribed by law.

7.4 On repayment of the Outstanding Balance in full, the Bank shall release all original documents of the Property and file for removal of the charge within 30 days, and the documents shall be returned at the Example Nagar Branch or at any other office of the Bank chosen by the Borrower.

## 8 Insurance

8.1 The Borrower shall keep the Property insured against fire, earthquake, flood and other natural calamities for not less than its reconstruction value throughout the tenor of the Loan, with the Bank noted as mortgagee.

8.2 The Borrower may, at his option, obtain life insurance cover for the Outstanding Balance. The Bank shall not compel the Borrower to obtain insurance from any particular insurer.

8.3 Copies of the current insurance policies and renewal receipts shall be furnished to the branch within 30 days of every renewal.

## 9 Pre-payment

9.1 The Borrower may pre-pay the Loan, in full or in part, at any time after giving seven days' written notice to the branch.

9.2 Part pre-payments shall be applied towards the principal outstanding. At the option of the Borrower, the EMI shall be reduced or the remaining tenor shall be shortened.

9.3 In the event of foreclosure or pre-payment of the loan, in full or in part, the Borrower shall pay a pre-payment charge of 3% of the principal outstanding.

9.4 On repayment of the Outstanding Balance in full, the Bank shall issue a no-dues certificate within seven working days and shall act in terms of clause 7.4.

## 10 Default and penal charges

10.1 The Borrower shall be in default if any EMI or other amount payable under this Agreement is not paid in full on its Due Date.

10.2 In the event of default in payment of any instalment, the Borrower shall pay penal charges of ₹500 per month of default; no interest shall be computed on penal charges.

10.3 Penal charges shall not be added to the rate of interest, shall be levied only on the amount in default and shall be disclosed in the KFS and in Schedule II. The Bank shall send a reminder to the Borrower before levying penal charges.

10.4 The levy of penal charges shall not affect the normal procedure for compounding of interest on the Loan.

## 11 Covenants of the Borrower

11.1 The Borrower shall use the Loan only for the purpose stated in clause 2.2 and shall furnish proof of end-use when required.

11.2 The Borrower shall not sell, lease, mortgage or otherwise dispose of the Property, or create any further charge on it, without the prior written consent of the Bank.

11.3 The Borrower shall pay all taxes, maintenance charges and outgoings in respect of the Property on time and shall keep the Property in good repair.

11.4 The Borrower shall inform the Bank in writing within 15 days of any change in his residential address, employment, business or contact details.

11.5 The Borrower shall permit officials of the Bank to inspect the Property at reasonable times after giving prior notice.

11.6 The Borrower shall not stand as guarantor for any other loan without informing the Bank.

11.7 The Borrower shall promptly inform the Bank of any legal proceedings that may affect the Property or his ability to repay the Loan.

11.8 The Borrower shall remain a member of the Bank until the Loan is repaid in full.

## 12 Events of default and remedies

12.1 Each of the following is an event of default: (a) non-payment of any EMI or other amount on its Due Date; (b) breach of any covenant or term of this Agreement; (c) any statement made by the Borrower being found to be materially incorrect; (d) the Property being attached, damaged or destroyed and not restored; (e) the Borrower being adjudged insolvent; (f) the Loan being used for a purpose other than that stated in clause 2.2; and (g) the death of the Borrower, unless his legal heirs agree to repay the Loan on the same terms.

12.2 On the occurrence of an event of default, the Bank may, after giving the Borrower not less than 30 days' written notice to remedy the default, recall the Loan and demand payment of the Outstanding Balance.

12.3 If the Outstanding Balance is not paid after demand, the Bank may enforce the security in accordance with the applicable law, including the law relating to enforcement of security interest.

12.4 The Bank shall follow its Fair Practices Code and Code of Conduct for recovery. The Borrower shall not be subjected to harassment, and recovery agents, if engaged, shall be informed to the Borrower in advance.

## 13 Disclosure of information

13.1 The Borrower agrees that the Bank may furnish information relating to the Loan and its repayment to credit information companies and to any authority as required by law.

13.2 The Borrower shall be informed before he is reported as a defaulter, and shall be given an opportunity to make payment.

## 14 Notices

14.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, by registered post or by e-mail to the address of the party stated in this Agreement or to any other address notified in writing.

14.2 A notice sent by registered post shall be deemed to have been delivered five days after posting.

## 15 Grievance redress

15.1 The Borrower may register any complaint with the Branch Manager of the Example Nagar Branch. If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the Borrower may approach the Grievance Redressal Officer at the Head Office of the Bank.

15.2 If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days by the Grievance Redressal Officer, or if the Borrower is not satisfied with the reply, the Borrower may approach the Reserve Bank of India under its Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

## 16 Miscellaneous

16.1 This Agreement, the sanction letter, the KFS and the Schedules together constitute the entire agreement between the parties in respect of the Loan.

16.2 No change to the terms of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and notified to the Borrower.

16.3 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Pune shall have jurisdiction.

16.4 This Agreement has been executed in duplicate. The Borrower acknowledges receipt of one original copy, together with a copy of every enclosure.
