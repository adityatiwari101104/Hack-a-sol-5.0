# Gold Loan FAQ

Example Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd.\
Frequently asked questions for customers\
Last updated: 15 January 2026

## 1 About the gold loan

1.1 What is a gold loan? A gold loan is a loan granted against the security of gold ornaments pledged with the Bank. It can be used for any personal or agricultural need, or for the working capital needs of a business.

1.2 Who can apply? Any member of the Bank who is at least 18 years of age and who owns the gold ornaments offered as security may apply. Nominal members may also apply.

1.3 What documents are needed? A recent photograph, proof of identity and proof of address are required. Existing customers whose KYC is up to date need only bring the ornaments.

## 2 Amount and tenure

2.1 How much can I borrow? The loan amount is a percentage of the assessed value of the gold ornaments, within the loan-to-value ceiling permitted under the applicable regulations. The branch will tell you the eligible amount after the ornaments are appraised.

2.2 How is the gold valued? The ornaments are tested and weighed by the Bank's approved appraiser in your presence. Only the gold content is valued; stones and other materials are not counted.

2.3 What is the tenure? Gold loans are granted for a period of up to 12 months. The loan may be renewed on maturity after a fresh appraisal of the ornaments.

2.4 How is interest charged? Interest is charged at the rate applicable on the date of the loan and is calculated on the daily balance. The current rates are displayed at the branch and on the website of the Bank.

## 3 Safety of the ornaments

3.1 Where are my ornaments kept? The ornaments are sealed in a packet in your presence and kept in the strong room of the branch, under joint custody.

3.2 Are the ornaments insured? Yes. The ornaments held by the Bank are covered under the Bank's insurance policy against theft, burglary and fire.

3.3 How do I get my ornaments back? The ornaments are returned to you, or to your authorised representative, on the same day the loan is closed, after you verify the seal of the packet.

## 4 Overdue loans and auction

4.1 What happens if I do not repay on time? The branch will send you reminders by SMS and letter. If the loan remains unpaid after maturity, the Bank may sell the ornaments by public auction to recover its dues.

4.2 Will I be told before an auction? Yes. The Bank will send you a written notice of auction at your registered address at least 14 days before the date of auction, giving the amount due and the date and place of the auction.

4.3 What happens to the surplus? If the auction proceeds exceed the amount due, the surplus will be paid to you. If they are less, you remain liable for the shortfall.
